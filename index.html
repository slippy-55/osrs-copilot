Suggestions for your free slots

Tap "Add to slot" to hold a flip in a free slot with its prices fixed. It stays there until you remove it or Dink sees you trade that item in game, then the slot follows your offer. Offer prices are 1 step above the instant-sell price and 1 step below the instant-buy price. Quantities assume you get about 10% of the item's trades while you wait, so real fills can be slower. Reliability looks at how much the item trades and how steady that's been over the last week.
